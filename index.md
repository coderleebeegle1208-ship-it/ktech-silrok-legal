# Ktech Silrok

Ktech Silrok is a Windows desktop application that creates short-form videos and lets the account owner upload or directly publish selected videos to their connected TikTok account.

The account owner controls whether publishing is enabled and chooses the video, caption, privacy level, and interaction settings. TikTok accounts are connected through TikTok’s official OAuth authorization process.
